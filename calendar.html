WK 1–2 (Ops Kickoff) is a real range, not two exact weeks — it's however long the ops project actually takes, which won't be clear until you see the pace in the room. Board Mtg 1 (Oct 2) is fixed and won't move: if the kickoff runs the full two weeks, WK 3–7 compress into whatever runway is left before Oct 2; if it wraps in one week, everything shifts up by one and there's a bit more room. Worth a gut-check against the real G-block dates once school starts, rather than assuming WK 1–7 fits exactly as drawn.

Everything else — the WK 1, WK 2, etc. day-to-day labels — are still relative placeholders, not real dates. The class actually meets roughly 2-3× per calendar week on the rotating G-block schedule (about 70+ sessions across the year), not once per week as this layout implies, so mapping every sprint/session to an exact date is a separate pass, not yet done here. Code-review cadence assumes roughly every 4 weeks (two sprints) starting once building begins in Winter Term, per your note. Phase 4 grading was deliberately padded with five distinct artifacts (deployment plan, go-to-market plan, bug-bash log, onboarding test, docs/security audit, handoff doc) since that's the stretch you flagged as short on gradable work last year.
